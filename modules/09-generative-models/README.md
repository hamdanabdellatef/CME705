# Generative and representation models

**Guiding question:** What can a model learn when reconstruction or data generation is the training signal?

## Learning outcomes

- distinguish discriminative and generative objectives;
- train an autoencoder;
- explain bottlenecks and reconstruction losses;
- describe the probabilistic change introduced by a VAE;
- evaluate representations or samples with appropriate limits.

## Topics

- latent variables
- autoencoders
- variational autoencoders
- generative modelling
- evaluation challenges

## Evidence of learning

A reconstructed batch, a latent representation, and an explanation of what reconstruction quality does and does not establish.

## Place in the course

Week 12 and the ablation or robustness result. Consult the [course schedule](../../schedule.md) for the associated lab and project milestone.
