## 16. Final author sign-off

- [ ] the title, abstract, talk, conclusion, and release state the same bounded claim;
- [ ] the central result is traceable to a command and fixed artifact;
- [ ] the talk distinguishes validation, test, and exploratory evidence;
- [ ] uncertainty is defined;
- [ ] limitations identify affected claims and consequences;
- [ ] the repository contains no credentials or prohibited data;
- [ ] the release manifest matches current files;
- [ ] the report and presentation have been exported and opened;
- [ ] the Week 13 correction remains in the final commit;
- [ ] the research direction contains competing explanations and a decision rule;
- [ ] the submission follows the institutional channel and deadline.
