# Research practice

**Guiding question:** How can an experiment support a precise claim and motivate the next research question?

## Learning outcomes

- search and compare relevant literature;
- separate hypotheses from observations;
- design baselines and controlled comparisons;
- record configurations and results reproducibly;
- identify threats to validity;
- propose a feasible next experiment.

## Topics

- research questions
- literature synthesis
- reproduction
- ablations and robustness
- scientific writing
- peer review and future directions

## Evidence of learning

A reproducible project whose conclusions match the evidence and whose next question is specific.

## Place in the course

Weeks 13–14 and the final project. Consult the [course schedule](../../schedule.md) for the associated lab and project milestone.
