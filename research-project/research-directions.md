# Research-direction note

Submit this note with the final project.

1. What did the evidence establish?
2. What important uncertainty remains?
3. What is the next experiment, stated precisely?
4. What result would change your current conclusion?
5. Which data, skills, compute, collaborators, or approvals are required?
6. Could this become a master's or PhD research direction? Explain its novelty, feasibility, and likely contribution without overstating them.

Possible pathways grounded in the course include efficient neural networks, computer vision, health-related data analysis, industrial condition monitoring, sequence modelling, and generative modelling. The pathway must follow from the problem and evidence.
