# Research project brief

Develop and investigate a feasible machine-learning question connected to an application or methodological interest. The project should demonstrate sound problem formulation, evidence-based model selection, a reproducible experiment, and a credible direction for continued research.

## Required components

1. **Problem and motivation:** define the prediction, representation, or analysis task; identify the unit of observation and intended use.
2. **Related work:** compare at least five relevant, credible sources. Identify methods, datasets, metrics, strengths, and limitations.
3. **Data:** document provenance, license or access conditions, population, labels, possible biases, preprocessing, and split construction.
4. **Baseline:** implement a simple reference method before a more complex model.
5. **Method:** justify the model and training procedure in relation to the problem and available data.
6. **Evaluation:** choose metrics and validation procedures before examining final test results. Prevent leakage and explain uncertainty.
7. **Results:** report your own results separately from published results. Include at least one controlled comparison, ablation, or robustness check.
8. **Limitations:** state failure modes, threats to validity, and conclusions the evidence does not support.
9. **Research direction:** propose the next experiment and the skills, data, or compute needed to continue.
10. **Reproducibility:** provide code, environment instructions, seeds, commands, and a compact results record.

## Deliverables

- a repository containing the code and reproduction instructions;
- a concise report of approximately 5–7 pages excluding references and appendices, developed with the [`report-template.md`](report-template.md);
- an 8-minute presentation focused on the question, evidence, and limitations, developed with the [presentation storyboard](presentation-template.md); and
- the milestone records listed in [`milestones.md`](milestones.md).

The final package follows [Assignment 6](../assignments/06-final-project-submission.md) and the [final-release checklist](final-release-checklist.md). Use figures and tables where they improve comparison or explanation. Every figure and result must identify its source or generating command. See [`rubric.md`](rubric.md) before selecting a project.
