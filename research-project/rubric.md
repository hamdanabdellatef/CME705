# Research project rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Strong evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Problem and justification | 20% | Specific question, appropriate ML formulation, credible motivation, feasible scope |
| Method and experimental design | 20% | Justified baseline/model, leakage-safe splits, controlled comparisons, appropriate metrics |
| Results and discussion | 20% | Clear own results, uncertainty/error analysis, limitations, comparison to prior work |
| Reproducibility and code | 30% | Fresh setup works, data steps documented, seeds/configurations recorded, outputs traceable |
| Research direction and communication | 10% | Concise report/presentation and a feasible, evidence-based next question |

Use the [presentation rubric](presentation-rubric.md) for the oral defense and the [final-release checklist](final-release-checklist.md) before assigning the final score.

## Essential conditions

A project cannot receive full credit if it lacks a baseline, uses test data for model selection, reports copied results as its own, omits the data source, or cannot explain how a displayed result was produced. Model complexity alone does not earn additional credit.
