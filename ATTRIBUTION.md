# Attribution and provenance

This first release reorganizes CME705 material taught at Karabuk University in Spring 2022. The instructor assumed responsibility for the course in Week 4 after an earlier instructor left. The structure and examples in this release were rewritten for a Python, NumPy, and PyTorch course route.

The historical working directory contains third-party textbooks, MIT 6.S191 slide decks, and source code distributed with Phil Kim's *MATLAB Deep Learning*. Those files are intentionally excluded from this release. In particular, the Phil Kim/Apress license contains restrictions on reuse in educational materials.

This repository links to official sources in [`readings/README.md`](readings/README.md). A link does not place the linked resource under this repository's license. Any future imported figure, dataset, or code sample must be recorded here with its author, source, license, and modifications.

Student submissions, grades, attendance, and identifying records are excluded. A student example may be added only with explicit permission, appropriate attribution, and review of its data and dependencies.

## Week 10 benchmark sources

The Week 10 ImageNet-1K comparison transcribes public accuracy, parameter, and GFLOP metadata for named weights from the [TorchVision 0.21 model table](https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/0.21/models.html). Dataset scale comes from the [official ImageNet download and access page](https://www.image-net.org/download.php). The research-frontier callout summarizes the reported ConvNeXt V2 Huge result from [Woo et al., “ConvNeXt V2: Co-designing and Scaling ConvNets with Masked Autoencoders”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00808). The transfer-learning reading follows the fixed-feature and fine-tuning distinction in the [official PyTorch transfer-learning tutorial](https://docs.pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/transfer_learning_tutorial.html) and uses the documented [ConvNeXt-Tiny TorchVision weights](https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/0.21/models/generated/torchvision.models.convnext_tiny.html). No third-party figures, datasets, or model weights are redistributed.

The reading guide links to original reporting frameworks and primary research; no third-party figures, datasets, code, or checklists are redistributed.
