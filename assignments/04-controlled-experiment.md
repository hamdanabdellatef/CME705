# Assignment 4: Controlled experiment

Change one factor in a working baseline, such as a learning rate, regularization method, feature set, architecture component, or data treatment. Hold other decisions constant. Record the hypothesis, configurations, repeated results or another uncertainty estimate, and conclusion.

**Deliverable:** runnable code, compact results table or figure, and 500–700 word analysis.
**Evidence assessed:** control of variables, reproducibility, appropriate metric, uncertainty, and restrained interpretation.
